What is AI red teaming?

Stress-testing AI before it reaches the world

Red teaming means adversarially probing an AI system to find risks before they cause real-life harms. Red-teamers intentionally surface unsafe outputs, biases, and other types of harms, drawing from military and cybersecurity traditions where simulated attacks reveal gaps early.

It's now a practice increasingly used at major tech companies and has even been encouraged by governments and public challenges like DEFCON 2023 and NIST's ARIA program. The challenge: there's an increasing need for red-teaming, but it's unclear how we can approach this challenge of scale while still retaining quality and centering the underlying human expertise necessary.
